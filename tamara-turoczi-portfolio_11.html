With a BA in Film and a Master's in Cinematography from Goldsmiths, University of London, Tamara brings a strong narrative sensibility shaped by years of experience in the HETV camera department and independent film.

Her work focuses on visually sophisticated storytelling, exploring the emotional and psychological complexity of human experience. She is drawn to character-driven narratives and creative collaborations that value atmosphere, nuance, and a distinct visual voice.

Tamara was a mentee of the BSC Mentorship Scheme (2024–2025). She works closely with directors, producers, and creative teams across short films, music videos, and branded content.

Owner-operator with Sony FX6 and Typoch Simera-C primes.
